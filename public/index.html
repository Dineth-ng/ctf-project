Operation NightHawk

Incident investigation scenario • Trainee orientation document

NexaCore Dynamics is a fictional technology research company developing an advanced autonomous cybersecurity system called Project Prometheus.

Incident Alert: A threat-actor group known as GhostByte has compromised NexaCore's network, claiming to have stolen source code and internal data related to Project Prometheus with a 48-hour deadline.

Participants act as Junior Penetration Testers and Threat Hunter trainees to investigate the attack and trace GhostByte's activities.

The investigation covers 6 progressive stages: OSINT discovery, cryptography decryption, web portal bypass, packet capture analysis, Linux escalation, and Prometheus vault decryption.

Final Objective

Reach the Prometheus Vault, investigate the system, and submit the final flag to complete the mission.
